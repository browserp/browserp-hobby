Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated 5 September 2026. BrowseRP processes the minimum information needed for accounts, listings, moderation and security.

Account information

When you choose Google sign-in, Google supplies your provider identifier, name, email address and profile image. When you choose Discord sign-in, Discord supplies your provider identifier, username, email address and avatar; BrowseRP requests your Discord community list only when a server-ownership check needs it. We use this information to create and secure your account, show your chosen identity, connect accounts at your request, communicate about account or moderation actions and verify server claims.

BrowseRP does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, Discord messages or unrelated provider data. Provider access and refresh tokens are used only for the requested sign-in or ownership-check flow and are never copied into operational logs.

Storage and service providers

Account, listing and moderation records are stored with Supabase. BrowseRP runs on Vercel and may use Cloudflare for domain and traffic protection. Google and Discord process the information involved when you choose their sign-in services. We share data with these service providers only as needed to operate and protect BrowseRP; we do not sell account information or use it for cross-site advertising.

Security information

For abuse prevention we use a random first-party device token, coarse browser, operating-system and device labels, a masked network range and keyed hashes. The full network address is separately encrypted. Staff see the masked range by default; an administrator reveal requires an owner-approved, time-limited request and every view is audited. We do not use invasive hardware, canvas or cross-site fingerprinting.

Retention and your choices

Accounts are warned after 45 days without recorded activity and become eligible for guarded deletion after 60 days. Active staff, accounts with financial records and accounts with unresolved uploaded media are excluded from automatic deletion. Removing an eligible account also removes its owned listings through the platform's account-deletion relationships. Security evidence may be pseudonymised or retained where needed for abuse prevention, legal obligations or appeals.

Security and moderation records are retained only as long as reasonably needed for abuse prevention, legal obligations and appeals. You may request access, correction or deletion subject to security, legal-hold and fraud-prevention exceptions. Account and moderation controls are available through your signed-in dashboard.
