Our vision

Make finding the right roleplay community simple.

BrowseRP is being built as a trusted starting point for FiveM players: one place to compare communities honestly, understand what they expect and move from browsing to joining without guessing.

What we want BrowseRP to become

Roleplay servers are not interchangeable. Region, rules, access, framework, economy, departments and community culture all change the experience. BrowseRP puts those details into a consistent directory instead of reducing every server to a banner and a vote count.

For players

Search and filters should make discovery quick. Full server pages, moderated comments, clear tags and reports should make decisions safer. Paid promotion is labelled and cannot bypass moderation or turn organic rankings into an auction.

For community owners

A listing should be a proper home for your community on BrowseRP: its own shareable address, useful facts, join routes, updates and a clear review history. Owners remain responsible for their communities and must be authorised to list them.

For the wider FiveM community

We know public server disputes can spill into TikTok and other social platforms. BrowseRP does not host harassment campaigns, retaliatory reporting, doxxing or community “beef”. Evidence-based reports are reviewed; coordinated abuse can result in removal and platform bans.
