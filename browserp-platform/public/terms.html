Using BrowseRP

BrowseRP does not own, operate or endorse listed servers unless a listing says so explicitly. Server owners are responsible for their communities, rules, staff, content and compliance.

  • You must be authorised to list a server and keep its information accurate.
  • Do not impersonate a community, manipulate votes, evade moderation or submit unsafe links.
  • Listings, profiles, comments, adverts and blogs must be lawful, accurate and respectful.
  • BrowseRP may review, request changes, suspend or remove content to protect users and the service.
  • Service availability is not guaranteed; planned and emergency maintenance may occur.

Accounts and sign-in

You are responsible for protecting access to the Google or Discord account you use to sign in. Do not share passwords, authentication codes or session details. Account linking and Discord ownership checks must use accounts you are authorised to control.

Moderation and safety

BrowseRP prohibits credential theft, malicious downloads, doxxing, threats, hate, sexual exploitation, targeted harassment, impersonation, ban evasion, vote manipulation and coordinated false reporting. Evidence-based reports are reviewed; using BrowseRP to continue community disputes or organise harassment can lead to restriction.

BrowseRP Coins and paid advertising are not currently for sale. Final purchase, delivery, cancellation and refund terms will be shown and reviewed before payments open.
