BrowseRP Coins

Coins will let verified listing owners activate clearly labelled boosts or time-limited animated logo and banner privileges. They are platform credits, not cryptocurrency, cash or an investment.

What coins will be used for

  • Time-limited featured placement on an eligible published server.
  • Animated listing logo or banner privileges after media review.
  • No safety bypass, guaranteed players or permanent ranking advantage.

Why purchases are disabled

The balance ledger exists, but BrowseRP will not take money until coin spending, receipts, refunds, partial refunds, disputes, reversals and reconciliation are tested end to end with Stripe. Existing pages must never imply a disabled feature is for sale.

Refund position

The final refund policy will be shown and accepted before checkout opens. Until then there are no coin sales and therefore no coin purchase to refund.
