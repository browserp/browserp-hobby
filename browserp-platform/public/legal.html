Terms, privacy and community rules.

What BrowseRP expects from people using the directory, and how the service handles the information needed to run it.

Last updated: 19 August 2026. BrowseRP currently uses essential authentication and security technology only. Payments, advertising and analytics are not active.

Terms of use

Use BrowseRP honestly and safely.

BrowseRP is a directory for finding FiveM roleplay communities. Server owners can submit listings for review and signed-in users can use account features made available by the service.

Your account

You are responsible for the third-party account you use to sign in. Do not impersonate another person or server owner, share access you are not authorised to share, evade access controls, or interfere with the service.

Listings and decisions

Information you submit must be accurate, current and authorised by the server owner. LCAPUK may request changes, reject, suspend or remove content that is unsafe, unlawful, misleading, abandoned or contrary to the community rules. An owner-verification label confirms control of the listing; it is not an endorsement or guarantee of the server.

Availability and outside services

The service may change, pause or withdraw features when needed for safety, maintenance or legal reasons. BrowseRP links to independent communities and does not control their availability, conduct or content. Use those services under their own rules and exercise normal care before downloading anything or sharing information.

Age and platform rules

You must meet the age and account requirements that apply to FiveM, Discord and any community you choose to join. BrowseRP does not override those requirements.

Privacy

Use only the information the service needs.

LCAPUK operates BrowseRP and is responsible for deciding how personal information is used for this service.

When you browse

Hosting and security systems process limited request information such as the requested page, time, browser details and network information. Application security records use reduced or hashed network signals where the system needs to recognise repeated abuse.

When you sign in or submit a listing

BrowseRP may process your sign-in provider identifier, display name, avatar, email address where supplied by the provider, profile settings, saved servers, submissions, moderation messages, notifications and security-relevant account activity. Authentication tokens and email addresses are not placed in public server listings.

Why it is used

  • to authenticate you and protect your account;
  • to review, publish and manage authorised server listings;
  • to save preferences and show relevant account updates;
  • to prevent spam, fraud and security abuse;
  • to review reports, appeals and moderation decisions; and
  • to comply with law and resolve disputes.

Providers

Vercel hosts BrowseRP. Supabase provides database and authentication infrastructure. Discord provides the current social sign-in route. A provider receives information needed to deliver its part of the service under its own terms and privacy documentation.

Retention and your rights

Information is kept only while it is reasonably needed for the account, listing, safety record or legal purpose for which it was collected. UK data-protection law may give you rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to processing of your personal information. It may also give you the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office.

Cookies

Essential operational cookies only.

BrowseRP does not currently use analytics cookies, advertising pixels or marketing cookies. It does not ask for a cookie choice when there are no optional cookies to accept or reject.

What the service may set

  • brp_access keeps the authenticated session active for about one hour.
  • brp_refresh renews that session for up to 30 days.
  • brp_csrf protects signed-in account and staff actions from cross-site requests.
  • brp_pkce, brp_auth_return, brp_auth_provider, brp_oauth_state and brp_oauth_nonce protect and complete sign-in. They expire after about 10 minutes or are removed when the sign-in attempt finishes.
  • __cf_bm and cf_clearance may be set by Cloudflare when its bot and abuse protections need to distinguish legitimate requests or remember a completed security challenge. Their duration is controlled by the security service and they are not used by BrowseRP for advertising.

In production the BrowseRP sign-in cookies use the secure __Host- prefix and are HTTP-only. These cookies provide authentication, service security and abuse prevention; they are not used to follow people across other websites. If optional analytics or advertising is introduced later, the policy and consent controls must be updated before that technology is switched on.

Community rules

Keep listings useful and safe.

Public listings and account content must be relevant to the FiveM community, accurate, and suitable for the audience the server claims to serve.

Do not submit or link to:

  • malware, credential theft, deceptive downloads or unsafe destinations;
  • impersonation, fabricated ownership or deliberately misleading server details;
  • hate, harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats or sexual exploitation;
  • spam, artificial engagement or attempts to manipulate directory order;
  • sale or promotion of unlawfully obtained material; or
  • content or activity that breaks applicable law or the rules of a relevant platform.

Automated checks may flag obvious risks. Consequential publication and moderation decisions use the protected staff process and include a recorded reason. Where an appeal route is available, eligible account holders can ask for another review.
