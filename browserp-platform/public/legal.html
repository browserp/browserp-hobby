Terms

Use BrowseRP fairly.

BrowseRP helps people find FiveM roleplay communities and lets authorised owners submit listings. You must provide accurate information, follow applicable law and avoid abusing the service or other people.

Accounts

You are responsible for keeping your sign-in account secure. Do not impersonate another person or server owner, attempt to bypass access controls, or use automated requests that interfere with the service.

Listings

We may review, request changes to, reject, suspend or remove a listing when it is misleading, unsafe, unlawful, abandoned or breaks the community rules. Owner verification confirms control of a listing; it is not a guarantee of a server’s quality.

Privacy

Collect only what the service needs.

When you browse without signing in, BrowseRP serves the directory and uses limited technical request information for security and abuse prevention. Network signals used by the application are hashed before they are stored.

If you sign in, the service may process your provider account identifier, display name, avatar, email address, profile settings, saved servers, listing submissions, notifications and account activity. Email addresses and authentication tokens are never shown on public profiles.

Why information is used

  • to sign you in and protect your account;
  • to publish and manage reviewed server listings;
  • to prevent spam, fraud and security abuse;
  • to operate moderation, reports and appeals; and
  • to meet legal obligations and resolve disputes.

Service providers

The current service uses Vercel for hosting, Supabase for database and authentication services, and Discord or Google when those sign-in options are enabled. Each provider processes information under its own terms and privacy commitments.

Your rights

People in the UK may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to use of their personal information, and to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office. A verified privacy contact route and retention schedule must be published before launch.

Cookies

Only essential sign-in cookies.

BrowseRP does not currently use analytics, advertising pixels or marketing cookies. The public pages do not write information to local or session storage.

Cookies used when you sign in

  • brp_access keeps the signed-in session active for roughly one hour.
  • brp_refresh securely renews the session for up to 30 days.
  • brp_pkce, brp_auth_return and brp_auth_provider protect and complete sign-in, then expire after 10 minutes or are removed immediately after use.

These cookies are HTTP-only and used for authentication or security requested by the user. They are not used to track people across websites. If optional analytics or advertising technology is added later, it must remain off until the visitor makes a clear choice.

Community rules

Keep listings safe and useful.

Listings and account content must be relevant, accurate and suitable for the audience they claim to serve. The following are not allowed:

  • malware, credential theft, dangerous downloads or deceptive links;
  • impersonation, fabricated ownership or misleading server information;
  • hate, harassment, discriminatory abuse or threats;
  • spam, artificial engagement or attempts to manipulate ranking; and
  • content or activity that breaks applicable law or platform rules.

Automated checks can flag obvious risks, but consequential moderation decisions are made through the protected staff process and recorded with a reason. Eligible decisions can be appealed.

Company and contact

Operator details.

BrowseRP is being developed as an LCAPUK project. Before public launch, this section and the site footer must show the operator’s exact registered legal name, company number, registered office address and a monitored privacy/contact address.
