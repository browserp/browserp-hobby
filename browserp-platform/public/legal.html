How BrowseRP operates

Terms of service

BrowseRP is a directory and moderation platform for roleplay communities. We do not own, operate or endorse listed servers unless a listing says so explicitly. Server owners are responsible for their communities, rules, staff, content and compliance.

  • You must be authorised to list a server and keep information accurate.
  • Do not impersonate a community, manipulate votes, evade moderation or submit unsafe links.
  • We may review, request changes, suspend or remove content to protect users and the service.
  • Availability is not guaranteed; planned and emergency maintenance may occur.

Privacy policy

BrowseRP processes the minimum information needed for accounts, listings, moderation and security. Google supplies a provider identifier, name, email address and profile image when you choose Google sign-in. Discord supplies a provider identifier, username, email address and avatar when you choose Discord sign-in; BrowseRP requests your Discord community list only when a server-ownership check needs it. We use this information to create and secure your account, show your chosen identity, connect accounts at your request, communicate about account or moderation actions and verify server claims.

BrowseRP does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, Discord messages or unrelated provider data. Provider access and refresh tokens are used only for the requested sign-in or ownership-check flow and are never copied into operational logs. Account, listing and moderation records are stored with Supabase. BrowseRP runs on Vercel and may use Cloudflare for domain and traffic protection. We share data with these service providers only as needed to operate and protect BrowseRP; we do not sell account information or use it for cross-site advertising.

For abuse prevention we use a random first-party device token, coarse browser/operating-system/device labels, a masked network range and keyed hashes. The full network address is separately encrypted. Staff see the masked range by default; an administrator reveal requires an owner-approved, time-limited request and every view is audited. We do not use invasive hardware, canvas or cross-site fingerprinting.

Accounts are warned after 45 days without recorded activity and become eligible for guarded deletion after 60 days. Active staff, accounts with financial records and accounts with unresolved uploaded media are excluded from automatic deletion. Removing an eligible account also removes its owned listings through the platform's account-deletion relationships; security evidence may be pseudonymised or retained where needed for abuse prevention, legal obligations or appeals.

Security and moderation records are retained only as long as reasonably needed for abuse prevention, legal obligations and appeals. Financial records will follow their statutory retention period if payments open. You may request access, correction or deletion subject to security, legal-hold and fraud-prevention exceptions. A focused copy of this policy is available at browserp.com/privacy.

Cookie policy

Current cookies are essential: secure session and refresh cookies, a CSRF protection token, short-lived OAuth state/PKCE cookies and a random first-party device-security token. Cloudflare may set strictly necessary security cookies when traffic is proxied or challenged. A light-or-dark display preference is stored locally in your browser only after you choose it; it is not sent to advertisers or used for profiling. BrowseRP currently uses no advertising trackers, cross-site profiling or non-essential analytics cookies, so there is no consent banner for optional cookies. This will change before any optional analytics or marketing cookie is introduced.

Community standards

Listings, comments, adverts, profiles and blogs must be lawful, accurate and respectful. BrowseRP prohibits credential theft, malicious downloads, doxxing, threats, hate, sexual exploitation, targeted harassment, impersonation, ban evasion, vote manipulation and coordinated false reporting.

We are aware that disputes between roleplay communities can spill into TikTok and other social platforms. BrowseRP does not tolerate using this directory to continue “beef”, organise harassment or retaliate against another server. Evidence-based safety reports are welcome; drama campaigns are not and can lead to permanent restriction.

Safety guidelines

  • Never share passwords, authentication codes, session tokens or private identity documents with a server listing.
  • Check that Discord and Cfx links use their normal official domains.
  • Report impersonation, unsafe downloads, harassment and misleading claims from the server page.
  • Do not assume an owner-verified badge is a guarantee of a community’s conduct.
  • Use the public appeal reference if you believe a BrowseRP restriction is wrong.

Refund policy

BrowseRP Coins and paid advertising are not currently for sale. Before payments open, checkout will show the exact digital service, price, delivery timing and applicable cancellation/refund terms. The production payment system must support failed fulfilment, expiry, full and partial refunds, disputes and ledger reversals before it can be enabled. This section will be replaced with the reviewed final policy before the first sale.

Advertising policy

Adverts are clearly labelled and placed in fixed top or side carousels. Campaigns must be relevant to roleplay communities and may not contain misleading claims, unsafe downloads, credentials, harassment or impersonation. Payment never bypasses content review, and adverts do not silently alter organic directory ranking.

Operator and contact

BrowseRP is an independent roleplay server directory operated in the United Kingdom. Account and moderation requests are handled through the signed-in account area. Restriction appeals are available at the public appeal page.
