### 2. FAQ — destination: `faq`

**What is BrowseRP?** A directory for finding roleplay communities and comparing the details that matter to you.

**How do I find or list a server?** Use **Discover** or **List a server** at https://www.browserp.com/. Each community sets its own joining requirements. Discord membership is separate from a listing application.

**Does a listing guarantee a good experience?** No. Read the community's rules, access requirements and current information before joining. An ownership check confirms control; it does not endorse everything a community does.

**Do game or Server Owner roles make someone staff?** No. Community roles and BrowseRP staff roles have different purposes.

### 4. Reports and appeals — destination: `help-desk` in Help & support

**Reporting a BrowseRP listing?** Use the report option on that listing's page. Explain what is wrong and include the relevant context.

**Appealing a BrowseRP website restriction?** Go to https://www.browserp.com/appeal and use the **BRP reference** shown with the restriction. Explain why the decision should be reconsidered. An appeal does not guarantee a reversal.

**Reporting a member here or appealing a Discord moderation decision?** The private Discord report and appeal route is still being prepared. Its instructions will be posted here when available. Do not publish accusations, private conversations or personal details in public chat.

**Posting note, not part of the message:** Website appeals and Discord moderation appeals are separate. Replace the pending Discord paragraph only after the real private route is available, with reports/appeals restricted to the appropriate reviewers. Do not send these cases to a general Support Team queue.

### 5. Suggestions and voting — destination: `ideas-and-votes`

**Help shape BrowseRP.** Use the private suggestion route linked here. Tell us what needs improving, who it affects and one example. Please keep account issues and member reports in their own help routes.

Staff review ideas before publishing them on this board. Approved ideas are open for constructive discussion and feedback through the voting option shown on the post. Focus on the idea, explain your view and avoid repeated submissions.

Each post's status explains what happens next: **Open for feedback**, **Under review**, **Planned**, **Completed** or **Declined**. Planned does not promise a delivery date. Votes help inform decisions; they do not guarantee a change or bypass listing standards and community rules.
