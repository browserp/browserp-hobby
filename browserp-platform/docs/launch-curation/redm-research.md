# RedM launch curation — 4 September 2026

Researched at 2026-09-04T01:42:42.997933+00:00. The accompanying `redm-candidates.json` contains 20 editorial candidates and four explicit holds. No listing was staged or published by this research.

## Method and practical limits

Each code was checked individually using the official Cfx single-server record. The importer rejects records unless `gamename` is explicitly `rdr3`; every recommended entry passed that check. English is supported by the listing locale and English community material, with stronger public-language rules where available. Source IPs, individual players and private Discord channels were not collected.

Community identity and usable onboarding were checked against the listing’s Discord invite and community-authored websites, rules, guides or Cfx forum posts. Public Discord invite resolution confirms the destination guild, **not ownership or staff quality**. Some communities publish most operational rules inside Discord; those gaps remain visible in the manifest. We did not join communities or inspect members.

Selection favors actual character roleplay, useful public information and a spread of settings: grounded western stories, beginners, text roleplay, medieval and supernatural communities. It is not a popularity ranking or a guarantee about moderation. Staff must review the current live source and the attached notes before publishing.

`status: publish` in the manifest means eligible for the explicit staff review flow. It does not mean published, verified, claimed or approved to skip confirmation. `region` remains **Not confirmed** because a language locale is not a verified hosting or community region. The access value **unknown** likewise remains explicit when contradictory or incomplete evidence prevents a defensible choice.

Counts below are historical observations on 4 September 2026 UTC. They must not be displayed as current after the freshness window. Zero is preserved only when actually reported in a usable observation. Offline/fallback or absent counts remain unavailable.

## Artwork checks

All 20 selected Cfx logo URLs resolved through the restricted image-fetch guard as PNGs under the 2 MB limit. Those URLs are attached to the exact selected code; this is source attribution, not permission or ownership verification. Static bytes were inspected for format and bounds. The Westhaven banner was additionally viewed and matched the community’s title and fantasy artwork.

The Rosalita, Gilded and Fremont Imgur URLs returned HTTP 200 and valid PNG bytes, but visual inspection showed a regional-unavailability placeholder. All three produced SHA-256 `faa24ec881e6040655c187a681d6dc496eb8aa41e1bd0652a180b3a40b457187`. They are deliberately omitted. No alternate proxy was used to bypass that response. Other expired attachments, failed image fetches and unsupported image hosts are likewise omitted.

Only Westhaven has a proposed banner. The remaining entries keep their verified Cfx icon and the site’s normal fallback artwork until staff or the owner supplies a suitable image. Detailed URL, MIME, size and invite checks are embedded per candidate in the manifest.

## Importer implementation and verification

The existing FiveM review/publish pipeline is generalized to a scoped Cfx adapter. Legacy FiveM routes remain available; RedM uses its own staff route and cannot publish a GTA record. VORP, RSG, RedEM:RP and QBR are recognized from known framework resources. Several exposed framework cores remain ambiguous rather than silently picking one.

Fetches stay on the fixed Cfx service endpoint, use a five-second timeout and bounded JSON, reject redirects, and do not visit server IP endpoints. RedM has no automatic featured/discovery import; staff paste reviewed codes or use the researched shortlist. Publishing still requires the existing staff permissions, MFA, versioned candidate review and explicit action. Owner-controlled metadata and claim state remain protected.
