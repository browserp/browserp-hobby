# Minecraft launch selection

Reviewed 4 September 2026. Three complementary English-speaking communities were selected using primary community material, joining information, RP focus and direct Minecraft status checks, not a ranking by population. All three public Discord invites resolved to the correct guild. No player samples or account identifiers were retained.

## Lord of the Craft

- Long-running dedicated character roleplay community
- Documented application process, rules and roleplay standards
- Current forum activity and successful direct status observation

Sources: [Current address, edition, established since 2011 and recent community announcements](https://www.lordofthecraft.net/), [Application requirements and official Discord invite](https://www.lordofthecraft.net/apply/new/?d=9&do=form), [Published standards for collaborative roleplay](https://www.lordofthecraft.net/forums/topic/188960-roleplay-quality-standards/)

Review notes: Website and server status advertise different version ranges; players should follow the current official joining guide. Network total, not the population of a particular nation or RP world.

## Stoneworks

- Distinct player-led geopolitical roleplay alongside character-focused selections
- Clear joining instructions and current official community channels
- Successful direct server status observation and matching server icon

Sources: [Official community identity, address, history, joining guide and FAQ](https://stoneworks.gg/), [Discord linked by the official website; verified guild Stoneworks MC](https://discord.com/invite/QhdhgbrF7F)

Review notes: Total covers the network rather than any one nation or RP world. RP intensity varies; the official FAQ welcomes casual participation.

## MassiveCraft

- Established fantasy roleplay and factions community adds a different play style
- Official operator and matching Discord identity can be corroborated
- Direct status is active and supplies its own valid icon

Sources: [Official operator description, advertised address and Discord invite](https://shop.massivecraft.com/), [Official invite verified as MassiveCraft Community](https://discord.gg/massivecraft), [Community homepage; automated reader receives 403, so current onboarding details require human review](https://www.massivecraft.com/)

Review notes: Current access requirements left Not confirmed rather than inferred from old directory advertisements. Homepage blocks automated reading; official store and Discord provide identity corroboration. Player count is network-wide, not isolated roleplay occupancy.

Direct Java status checks returned valid counts and 64px PNG icons at the publicly advertised endpoints. These observations expire after five minutes; they are not frozen marketing numbers. Production verification records the final observed state separately.
