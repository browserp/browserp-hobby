# RedM selection follow-up — quality and normal-session activity

Reviewed 4 September 2026. This is an editorial comparison and read-only source audit, not a staff certification or publication instruction. It supplements [the original 23-community review](redm-minecraft-quality-review-2026-09-04.md). No accounts, joins, messages, production records or source-refresh actions were changed.

## Decision

**No newly researched alternative is ready for unconditional publication under all the current requirements.** Two are worthwhile next reviews: Wanted RP: The Last Ride and New Eden. They have substantially stronger repeated activity than the weakest activity fits in the present roster, but the readable evidence does not yet establish their current reporting/appeal procedures and welcoming staff practice. Wanted also currently returns an unavailable Cfx observation. Do not turn these remaining checks into invented assurances.

The five alternatives examined were Wild West RP, WildRP, GoldRush, New Eden and Wanted RP. Keep the distinction between community quality, application requirements, activity and reliable import identity. Strong governance does not make a 17-player weekly peak satisfy the user's approximate 35-player active-session requirement; a busy world does not establish fair moderation.

**Recommended present action:** retain the stronger existing communities; keep Medieval, Souvenir Trails, Highwater and Fremont after the seven-day evidence resolves misleading off-peak readings. Put Lemoyne first for replacement consideration on activity. Sanctuary & Sin is second because it combines near-floor activity, very recent launch and thin independent governance evidence. Treat Everglade as a near-threshold watch with good public onboarding, not an automatic removal for being a few players below 35. Bright Falls remains a priority governance review, not a proven misconduct finding.

### Wild West RP — excellent documented structure; reserve for smaller-community use

The [current rules](https://wildwestrp.com/threads/wild-west-rp-rules-guidelines.3/) have a revision trail beginning 2019, adult English voice requirements, speech exceptions, harassment/discrimination prohibitions, immediate reports for zero-tolerance incidents and consent that can be withdrawn. The [FAQ updated 11 February 2026](https://wildwestrp.com/ewr-carta/faqs/) explains applications, technical setup, first steps and accessible communication. The [government discussion](https://wildwestrp.com/threads/government-rework-poll.42811/) began 20 June 2026 with replies through August; staff solicits constructive input about player political roles. This is visible development/governance participation, not proof every support interaction is good. A [January 2026 player account](https://www.reddit.com/r/RDR2RP/comments/1q2l3cn/looking_for_server_recommendations/) says it is mostly good but quiet, consistent with this week's peak 17. **Do not add for the current35-player brief.**

### WildRP — strong specialist RP; current code is a test realm

The website uses its own launcher.

## Preferred 20 plan and gates

A defensible provisional 20 keeps these 18 existing communities: Ranch; Syn County; State of Deliverance; Bright Falls; Ebonridge; The Frontier; True Grit; Medieval; Rosalita; Gilded; Dust to Dreams; Still Water; Souvenir Trails; Everglade; Western Skies; Highwater; Westhaven; Fremont. The candidate replacement pairs are:

1. **Sanctuary & Sin → Wanted RP: The Last Ride**, conditional on governance review and source readiness.
2. **Lemoyne → New Eden**, conditional on governance review and honest entry-method labelling.

This is **a review plan, not 20 fully qualified recommendations or instructions to publish**. If those two gates cannot be satisfied, keep the two candidates in draft and state that 18 retained plus 2 candidate reviews remain; do not fill the quota with WildRP's test realm or WWRP's currently much smaller community. Existing thinly documented listings are retained pending review rather than branded poor merely because their rules are Discord-only. Bright Falls remains the most pressing governance check: multiple negative player accounts justify scrutiny, but neither a paid-appeal amount nor abusive moderation was independently verified. No allegation is promoted to fact here.

Within the retained 18, the highest editorial confidence remains Ranch, Syn, Ebonridge, True Grit, Rosalita, Gilded, Western Skies and Westhaven, with Everglade's good onboarding balanced against near-floor activity. State of Deliverance has encouraging player feedback and concrete developer work; Medieval adds distinctive setting/systems with a policy caveat; Souvenir has documented development/onboarding history. Frontier, Dust, Still Water, Highwater and Fremont have meaningful activity but more limited independently readable governance evidence. That difference should remain visible internally rather than pretending all 20 were equally audited.
