## Legit RP — application-based US storytelling

**Positive case.** The [official homepage](https://legitrp.gg/) links its Connect button to `ex853d` and its Discord button to `LegitRP`. It presents civilian businesses, emergency services, government and criminal stories rather than only combat. The [operator's July2025 Cfx introduction](https://forum.cfx.re/t/legit-roleplay/5339313) explicitly advertises application-based admission and the same Discord. These provide identity and history; they are operator statements, not independent recommendations. A current source flag also agrees with the application label.

**Conduct and friction.** The [public rules](https://legitrp.gg/rules) require English,18+ and a working microphone, prohibit harassment and discrimination, require consent from everyone involved in sensitive scenes, and protect against random robbery and forced character deletion. Reports require video footage and are directed to Discord after the scene. Banned players are removed from Discord; a separate public appeal route was not recovered. These are meaningful limitations for newcomers without recording tools, not proof that staff disregard genuine complaints. The private enforcement record was not inspected. Avoid describing the server as universally safe, easy to appeal or equally accessible to everyone.

The [community's account of its founding values](https://legitrp.gg/mike) emphasises helping people learn and form connections. Treat this as the operator's stated mission, without reproducing personal family details. Current recruitment crossposts repeat marketing copy; they are not independent endorsements. Old `LegitRP.com` complaints from2018/2021 were not attributed to the present `legitrp.gg` identity and should not be borrowed as evidence against it.

**Editorial judgement:** a defensible curated listing, with current activity and clear participation standards. More detailed independent player-experience evidence would improve confidence, but its absence does not erase the concrete published safeguards and continuing community history.

## Summit RP — strong public-entry alternative

**Positive case.** The [official connection guide](https://summitrp.gg/connect) explicitly says general admission is public. Players join Discord, claim the Civilian role, read the rules, connect and create their character. It gives the address and several connection methods, while explaining that Discord membership remains necessary. Do not mislabel the server Whitelisted because the feed retains a `soft whitelist` tag or individual careers need approval.

The [public terms, updated13July2026](https://summitrp.gg/terms), specify18+, prohibit discriminatory harassment and sexual harassment, forbid ERP and several abusive RP themes, and require consent for torture scenes. Players report through Discord tickets, with an emergency exception to the normal no-staff-DM rule. People with speaking difficulties can request alternative roleplay arrangements. The terms also disclose broad staff discretion and non-refundable purchases. These are concrete participation safeguards and limitations, not proof of consistent enforcement.

The linked [full server rule document](https://docs.google.com/document/d/10PcUBXVYPyec7sU97YQAbQXxScmVV_ITraSHbyM0CNg/edit) returned a JavaScript shell; ordinary export access required sign-in. No access control was bypassed. The public terms provide enough conduct evidence for a proportionate recommendation, although the full rulebook remains an explicit review limit.

**Evidence of community life.** The [announcements page](https://summitrp.gg/announcements) includes a July2026 joint medical-service/café blood-drive event, a concrete civilian interaction rather than simply a feature count. The [review page](https://summitrp.gg/reviews) explicitly calls its July2026 testimonials hand-picked. Several describe first-time RP help, long-term friendships and responsive staff; these are selected self-reports, not an independent satisfaction score. A [2025 Cfx help request](https://forum.cfx.re/t/issues-with-loading-into-a-server/5319299) describes a player's loading difficulty; it is neither a confirmed current outage nor proof that the problem was fixed.

**Purchasing caveat.** Current [Diamond priority](https://summitrp.gg/store/package/6873119) includes vehicle-related benefits, and an [S-class vehicle package](https://summitrp.gg/store/package/7434723) is offered separately. Do not call purchases cosmetic-only, promise no gameplay advantage or recommend spending as a prerequisite. This does not automatically disqualify an otherwise useful free-entry directory listing, but its monetisation should not be concealed by promotional claims.

**Editorial judgement:** particularly useful to balance application-only cities with a documented immediate-entry route. There is stronger visible onboarding than a generic Discord invite, credible current activity, and public behavioural protections. Actual moderation outcomes and in-game performance were not audited.
