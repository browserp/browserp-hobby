Recommend **The Tackroom (`ov754y`)** to replace Lemoyne County. It clears the activity threshold and has usable public onboarding and conduct evidence. Describe it accurately as an **18+ equestrian sandbox with optional roleplay**. It should not be listed as strict narrative or serious RP. The adjacent JSON contains the reviewed import fields; no publication or production changes were made during this research.

That gives it a distinct place alongside the more restrictive story-focused communities. The [official rule page](https://thetackroom-redm.com/the-rules/) embeds a [public 2026 rulebook](https://docs.google.com/document/d/e/2PACX-1vRJR0tiC-ZkFBEvKrXXWpIW7NlcaScFTNNn24HNj_GMn1R6-wHS09XS7r5IXvvLBX8JDJCBnjmp5c41/pub?embedded=true), fetched without authentication. It requires English in public channels and kindness to newcomers; prohibits harassment and discrimination; protects accessibility features; and requires consent for PvP. Separate ticket routes cover player reports, staff reports and ban appeals. These are concrete support procedures, not a guarantee of actual staff conduct. The rules also forbid discussion/promotion of other communities or products even when positive, a material restriction worth understanding before joining.

The [team page](https://thetackroom-redm.com/meet-the-team/) identifies moderation, player-experience, event and housing support functions. Current [paid support offerings](https://thetackroom-redm.tebex.io/) include exceptions to ordinary housing eligibility/activity requirements; do not make a “no paid benefits” claim.
